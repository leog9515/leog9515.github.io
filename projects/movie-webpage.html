Movie webpage project

The prompt for this project was to create a webpage for a fake movie with your partner

I this project me and my partner made a project for a fake Spider-man movie with multiple images as well as what the plot of the fake movie would be

Some challenges I faced when making this project with my partner was the lack of comunicatioon which leads into my take away where I realized how important is was to communicate with your partner and how it can really affect how well the project does. This made me realize that moving forward I would have to communicate better and more with my teamates or else it might turn out not as good as expected.
